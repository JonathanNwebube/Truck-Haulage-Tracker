AETR Rules


Driving Hours

  • Musn't drive more than 9 hours in a day
    • Can extend this to 10 hours twice a week
  • Musn't drive more than 56 hours in a week
  • Musn't drive more than 90 hours in any 2 consecutive weeks

Must record all driving you do under EU rules on a tachograph


Breaks and Rest

  • Must have at least 11 hours rest every day
    • Can reduce this to 9 hours rest 3 times between any 2 weekly rest periods
  • Must take an unbroken rest period of 45 hours every week
    • Can reduce this to 24 hours every other week
  • Must take a break or breaks totalling at least 45 minutes after no more than 4 hours 30 minutes driving
  • Must take a weekly rest 6 consecutive 24-hour periods of working, starting from the end of the last weekly rest period taken
